# Incorporation of utPLSQL

For many years, utPLSQL has been built and maintained through the contributions of dedicated and passionate engineers from the community.

To provide a sustainable legal structure for the project, utPLSQL is now transitioning under the legal custody of **utPLSQL Development Labs Ltd**.
<!-- more -->

The incorporation does **not** change the open-source nature of utPLSQL. All existing utPLSQL projects remain free to use and open source, licensed under the Apache License 2.0.

The company has been established primarily to provide the infrastructure needed to sustain and grow the project. In particular, it will allow us to:

* establish a legal entity that can hold the project's intellectual property, name and branding, providing continuity as maintainers and contributors change;
* manage bookkeeping, finances, contracts, invoices, sponsorships and other arrangements with supporters;
* support active maintenance and development of utPLSQL and help the project grow to its full potential;
* make it possible to fund dedicated development work where appropriate.

**Sponsorship or financial support does not provide ownership of utPLSQL or control over its direction.** Contributions to the project remain open to the community.

## Roadmap

Alongside this transition, I have published a roadmap outlining the major architectural and feature initiatives planned for utPLSQL. The roadmap is intended to provide visibility into the direction of the project and serve as an entry point for discussions around individual initiatives and their implementation.

See the [utPLSQL Roadmap on GitHub](https://github.com/orgs/utPLSQL/discussions/15).

The goal is to provide utPLSQL with a more sustainable foundation while preserving its open-source nature and community-driven development.

For a more personal perspective on the history behind this decision, the motivations for establishing utPLSQL Development Labs Ltd, and the thinking behind this next chapter for utPLSQL, I have written a more detailed post on [oraclethoughts.com](https://oraclethoughts.com/blog/2026/09/25/incorporation_of_utPLSQL/).
